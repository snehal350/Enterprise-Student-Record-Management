# Project Charter

## Project Title
Enterprise Student Record and Academic Management Platform

## Purpose
To plan a software solution for organizing student records and academic information in a higher education institution.

## Objectives
- Understand the existing academic management process.
- Identify stakeholders and their requirements.
- Define the product vision and project scope.
- Prepare functional and non-functional requirements.
- Define the Minimum Viable Product (MVP).
- Prepare an initial system architecture and product roadmap.

## Scope
The project covers student registration, student record management, attendance management, examination results, and academic report generation at the planning stage.

## Stakeholders
- Students
- Faculty members
- Academic administrators
- Examination cell staff
- IT support personnel

## Risks
- Incomplete requirements
- Data privacy and security concerns
- Delays in gathering stakeholder feedback
- Integration challenges with existing systems

## Expected Outcome
A documented product plan ready for Agile planning and subsequent development units.
