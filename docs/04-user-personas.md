# User Persona Report

## Persona 1: Student
**Name:** Aarav — Representative Persona

**Goals**
- Access academic records
- Check attendance and examination results
- Obtain academic reports when required

**Challenges**
- Delays in obtaining information
- Difficulty tracking academic records
- Dependence on manual processes

**Expectations**
- Easy navigation
- Accurate information
- Secure access

## Persona 2: Faculty Member
**Name:** Priya — Representative Persona

**Goals**
- Record student attendance
- Maintain academic information
- Access class-related records

**Challenges**
- Repetitive data entry
- Time-consuming record maintenance
- Errors in manual records

**Expectations**
- Simple interfaces
- Efficient attendance entry
- Reliable student information

## Persona 3: Academic Administrator
**Name:** Rahul — Representative Persona

**Goals**
- Maintain student records
- Generate academic reports
- Coordinate academic administration

**Challenges**
- Managing large amounts of information
- Retrieving records quickly
- Maintaining data consistency

**Expectations**
- Centralized records
- Accurate reports
- Appropriate access controls

Note: These are hypothetical personas for initial product planning and should be validated with actual users.
