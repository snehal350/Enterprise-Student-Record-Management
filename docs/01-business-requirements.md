# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Project Title
Enterprise Student Record and Academic Management Platform

## 2. Introduction
The proposed platform aims to organize student records and academic information within a higher education institution. It focuses on improving the management of admissions, course registration, attendance, examinations, results, and academic reports.

## 3. Business Problem
Manual academic record management can create difficulties in maintaining accurate records, retrieving information quickly, coordinating academic activities, and generating reports. The proposed platform aims to address these challenges through a centralized software solution.

## 4. Business Objectives
- Maintain organized student records.
- Simplify student registration and course information management.
- Support attendance tracking.
- Improve examination result management.
- Generate academic reports efficiently.
- Protect academic information through appropriate access controls.

## 5. Stakeholders
- Students
- Faculty members
- Academic administrators
- Examination cell staff
- IT support personnel

## 6. Proposed Solution
Develop a centralized platform that stores and manages student and academic information. Authorized users will be able to access the functions relevant to their responsibilities.

## 7. Business Requirements
- BR-01: The system should maintain student registration records.
- BR-02: The system should organize student academic information.
- BR-03: Faculty members should be able to record attendance.
- BR-04: Authorized examination staff should manage examination results.
- BR-05: The system should generate academic reports.
- BR-06: Access to academic information should be controlled according to user roles.

## 8. Project Scope

### In Scope
- Student registration
- Student record management
- Attendance management
- Examination result management
- Academic report generation

### Out of Scope for the Initial Planning Phase
- Production deployment
- Integration with external university systems
- Online fee payment
- Advanced predictive analytics

## 9. Assumptions
- The institution will provide the required academic information.
- Different users will have different access permissions.
- Stakeholder feedback will be collected during product discovery.

## 10. Success Criteria
- All major stakeholder groups are identified.
- Business requirements are documented clearly.
- The initial MVP scope is defined.
- Product planning documents are prepared for subsequent development.

## 11. Conclusion
The proposed platform aims to improve the organization and accessibility of academic information. This BRD provides an initial foundation for requirements analysis and future Agile development.
