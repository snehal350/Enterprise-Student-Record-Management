# Stakeholder Analysis Report

| Stakeholder | Responsibilities | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Students | View personal and academic information | Accurate records and easy access |
| Faculty | Record attendance and academic information | Simple data entry and class records |
| Academic Administrators | Manage student information | Centralized records and reports |
| Examination Cell | Manage examination results | Accurate result processing |
| IT Support | Maintain the platform | Security, reliability, and maintainability |

## Stakeholder Priorities
- Students: Accessibility and accuracy
- Faculty: Ease of use
- Administrators: Efficient record management
- Examination Cell: Reliable results
- IT Support: System security and stability

## Conclusion
Stakeholder analysis helps ensure that the proposed platform addresses the needs of everyone involved in academic management.
