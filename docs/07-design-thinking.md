# Design Thinking Report

## Introduction
Design Thinking is a user-centered approach to understanding problems and developing appropriate solutions.

## Stage 1: Empathize
Understand the experiences of students, faculty members, administrators, and examination staff through interviews, observations, and feedback.

## Stage 2: Define
**Problem Statement:** Academic information may be difficult to maintain, retrieve, and update when records are managed through disconnected or manual processes.

## Stage 3: Ideate
Possible solutions include:
- Centralized student records
- Digital attendance management
- Organized examination result records
- Automated academic report generation
- Role-based access

## Stage 4: Prototype
Prepare low-fidelity wireframes for the student dashboard, attendance page, result page, and administrative dashboard.

## Stage 5: Test
Ask representative users to evaluate the prototypes. Record usability problems and improve the proposed design.

## Conclusion
The five stages help develop a solution based on user needs rather than assumptions alone.
